    MedRTC.PeerLoopback (med_ex v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Minimal real ExWebRTC.PeerConnection SDP/ICE loopback harness used to exercise live WebRTC negotiation, peer disconnect, and replacement connection establishment against MedRTC.SessionServer.

    Starts two real ExWebRTC.PeerConnection instances with an :audio transceiver, exchanges SDP offer/answer and ICE candidates, and waits until both peers reach :connected.
