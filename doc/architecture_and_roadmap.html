MedEx Workspace
v0.1.0

    Architecture and roadmap

    Copy Markdown View Source

    MedEx provides real-time clinical media capture and WebRTC preview transport for Elixir applications. Host applications own authorization, encounter state, clinician review, persistence, and healthcare record export—MedEx has no database, Phoenix server, or FHIR dependency.

    Why two separate media paths?

    Clinical diagnostic audio (such as remote auscultation) has conflicting requirements:

    • Live listening needs low-latency, resilient WebRTC Opus streaming that can drop packets or reconnect peers across network hiccups.
    • Authoritative recording needs bit-exact, continuous, lossless PCM audio whose integrity is never compromised by WebRTC jitter, Opus compression, or clinician browser disconnects.

    MedEx enforces this split in its process boundaries: live indexed PCM enters MedEx.Capture, which writes the lossless WAV directly and fans full 20 ms frames out to a bounded, disposable preview branch (Membrane.Opus.Encoder kept private behind MedEx.Capture → MedEx.Preview over ExWebRTC).

    flowchart LR
  PCM["Indexed PCM16LE (48 kHz)"] --> Capture["MedEx.Capture (Lossless Writer)"]
  Capture --> WAV["Immutable &lt;sha256&gt;.wav + MedEx.Evidence"]
  Capture -. "bounded 1-frame demand" .-> Encoder["Private Membrane Opus Encoder"]
  Encoder --> Peer["MedEx.Preview (ExWebRTC)"]
  Peer --> Listener["Clinician Browser (Replaceable)"]

    Process ownership and failure domains

    ComponentOwnerFailure behavior
    MedEx.CaptureHost DynamicSupervisor (restart: :temporary, no global names)Monitors the :owner producer PID; aborts and deletes its .part file if the producer exits before finish/1.
    Preview encoder pipelinePrivate to MedEx.Capture (Membrane.Opus.Encoder, application: :audio)Isolated via trapped exits; if the encoder exits, capture notifies the subscriber with {:med_ex_preview_failed, pid, reason} and keeps recording.
    MedEx.Preview peerHost signaling channel processPeer drop, disconnect, or replacement only changes the preview subscriber; capture continues uninterrupted.
    MedEx.EvidenceReturned to host on finish/1Carries kind: :audio_pcm16_wav, content-addressed artifact_key, separate file_sha256 and pcm_sha256, sample bounds, and continuity verdict.

    Non-audio modalities (multi-channel ECG/PPG waveforms, video tracks)PlannedWill define modality-specific capture and evidence contracts alongside kind: :audio_pcm16_wav rather than overloading PCM audio fields.
    Out-of-band signal analysis & inference workers (NeuroKit2, WFDB-Python, ONNX, voice AI)PlannedSeparately installed/deployed packages or workers consuming finalized MedEx.Evidence outside capture and emitting derived results with source digest and algorithm provenance.
    Multiparty SFU topologies (e.g. LiveKit) & managed TURN deploymentsPlannedExternal media services integrated when a product topology requires multiparty forwarding or NAT relay.
